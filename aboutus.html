About the Creator

A solo journey of passion, learning, and dedication to Islamic knowledge

My Journey

Assalamu Alaikum! I'm the sole creator behind Noor Al Huda, and this platform is the result of a personal journey that began with a simple desire: to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible to everyone, everywhere.

With no formal background in web development, I embarked on a self-taught journey to learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. What started as curiosity turned into a passion project that would eventually become Noor Al Huda. Every line of code, every design decision, and every feature you see on this platform was crafted by hand, driven by the vision of creating something meaningful for the Shia community.

This project represents countless hours of learning, experimenting, and refining. From understanding the basics of web development to implementing complex features, each step was a learning experience that brought me closer to realizing this vision.

The Learning Path

Self-Taught Developer

Started from scratch, learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through online resources, documentation, and countless hours of practice. Every error was a lesson, every success a milestone.

First Deployment - Netlify

Initially deployed the website on Netlify, learning about continuous deployment, custom domains, and web hosting. This was my first taste of seeing my work live on the internet.

Migration to GitHub

Transitioned to GitHub Pages for better version control, collaboration potential, and integration with the developer community. Learned Git, version control, and modern development workflows.

Design & UX

Taught myself design principles, user experience, and accessibility standards to ensure the platform is not just functional, but beautiful and easy to use for everyone.

"This project is more than just a website, it's a labor of love, a commitment to serving the Shia community, and a testament to what one person can achieve with dedication, faith, and the right tools."
