About Noor Al Huda

Discover the comprehensive Islamic knowledge platform designed to illuminate your spiritual journey

What is Noor Al Huda?

Noor Al Huda, meaning "Light of Guidance," is a comprehensive digital platform dedicated to providing authentic Islamic knowledge and resources to Muslims worldwide. Our mission is to make Islamic knowledge, Quranic studies, and spiritual guidance accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or background.

Built with modern technology and rooted in traditional Islamic knowledge, we bridge the gap between classical Islamic texts and contemporary digital accessibility. Our platform serves as a beacon of authentic knowledge, carefully curated to ensure accuracy and authenticity in all our resources.

What We Offer

Noor Al Huda provides a comprehensive suite of Islamic resources and tools:

Quranic Studies

Complete Quran with multiple translations and audio recitations from renowned Qaris.

Hadith Collections

Extensive database of authentic hadiths from major collections including Al-Kafi, Man la Yahduruhu al-Faqih, and other trusted Shia sources. Each hadith is carefully verified and includes chain of narration details.

Duas and Ziyarahs

Comprehensive collection of daily duas, special occasion prayers, and supplications from the Ahlulbayt tradition, including famous duas like Dua Kumayl, Dua Tawassul, and Ziyarah Ashura.

Prayer Resources

Accurate prayer times for locations worldwide, Qibla direction finder, Islamic calendar with important dates.

AI-Powered Learning

With Multiple AI tools to ask questions and understand shia beliefs and practices more deeply.

Why Choose Noor Al Huda?

Noor Al Huda is built to provide reliable, accessible access to authentic Islamic sources in one place. Our focus is not entertainment or trends, but preserving and presenting knowledge directly from the Qur’an, Ahl al-Bayt (ʿalayhim al-salām), and trusted classical books.

Instead of scattered PDFs or difficult websites, you get structured libraries, searchable hadith collections, translations, and study tools which are designed for serious learning, research, and daily reflection. Everything is organised, so students, teachers, and families can quickly find what they need without distraction.

If you want clarity, authenticity, and a clean space to study Islam properly, Noor Al Huda is made for you. A simple hub for knowledge, remembrance, and growth.
